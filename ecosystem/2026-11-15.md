- **Decimo:** decimal rounding/overflow oracle; separate conversion from arithmetic.
- **duckdb.mojo:** FFI boundary vs engine work, avoid mislabeling DuckDB performance as pure Mojo.
- **SciJo:** analytical derivative vs finite-difference error at multiple step sizes.
