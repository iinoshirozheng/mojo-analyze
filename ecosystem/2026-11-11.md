### A01 — Inductive enums and structural recursion
**Author:** Anatoly Vitold Stankyavichyus; **lane:** October 2026 technical backlog, discussion active in November. [Original proposal](https://forum.modular.com/t/companion-proposal-inductive-enums-and-structural-recursion/3570). Criticism highlights separating type-level guarantees from heap/RC policy. **Status:** proposal plus library prototype, not implemented compiler guarantees. **Research angle:** compare iterative worklist traversal and explicit-box recursion for correctness, allocation and peak stack.
